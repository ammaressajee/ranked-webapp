How Ladders Works

Join leagues, find fair matches, log results, and climb the ranks.

info How the app works

Ladders is a competitive ladder app where you join leagues, get matched with opponents at a similar skill level, and record match results to move up (or down) the leaderboard. Your rank in each league is based on wins and losses. The more you play, the more accurate your rank becomes.

  • Sign in with Google to get started.
  • Browse and join one or more leagues (e.g. your local club or region).
  • From a league page, use Find Match to get paired with someone.
  • After the match, one player submits the score; the other confirms it.
  • Ranks update automatically and you can view the leaderboard anytime.

group Matchmaking

When you tap Find Match in a league, the app looks for other players in that league who are also searching. To keep games fair, we use skill-based matchmaking: you are matched with opponents whose rank is close to yours.

Expanding search window — If no one is available within a tight rank range, the allowed rank difference grows the longer you wait (e.g. first minute: within 150 points; after a few minutes: wider range). So you still get a match without waiting forever, while early matches stay balanced.

Once you’re matched, your opponent must accept the match. After they accept, you can play and then submit the score (see below).

edit_note Logging your score

After a completed match, either you or your opponent can submit the score from My Matches. Enter the correct result (who won and the score if your league uses it).

  • The other player must confirm the result. Until they do, the match stays “Awaiting confirmation”.
  • If the submitted score is wrong, the opponent can decline; you can then submit again or discuss.
  • Once both agree, the match is completed and both players’ ranks and stats update.

You can also cancel a match (e.g. if it won’t be played). Cancelled matches don’t affect your rank.

request_quote Requesting leagues

Leagues are created and managed by admins. If you don’t see a league for your area or club, you can request one from the Browse Leagues page.

  • Use the “Request a league” (or similar) option and fill in the league name and any details.
  • An admin will review the request and can create the league and add you as a member.
  • Once the league exists, you and others can join and start finding matches.

If you run a club or community and want to become an admin, contact the app owner to get admin access so you can create and manage leagues.

Ready to play? Pick a league and hit Find Match.
